Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how NOVA may handle information when you use the NOVA website, applications and related services. It is intended to describe the practices that apply to the current product and may be updated as NOVA changes.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on the features you use, NOVA may receive information you provide directly, such as account details, settings, messages or support requests. Technical information such as device, browser, operating system and diagnostic information may also be processed when necessary to operate and secure the service.

3. AI and voice features

AI prompts, responses and voice data may be processed when you use those features. The exact processing depends on the NOVA version and the AI services configured for it. NOVA should not be represented as private or offline for a particular feature unless that feature actually operates that way.

4. How information is used

Information may be used to provide features, authenticate users, maintain security, troubleshoot problems, improve reliability and respond to support requests.

5. Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers or infrastructure necessary to operate a requested feature, or where required by law. NOVA should not sell personal information merely because analytics or third-party infrastructure is used.

6. Retention and deletion

Retention depends on the type of information and the feature involved. Where account or data deletion controls are provided, users can use those controls or contact NOVA for a request.

7. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children and young users

NOVA should be used in accordance with applicable age requirements and local law. Features involving younger users should be configured with appropriate safeguards before release.

9. Changes

This policy may change as NOVA adds features, platforms or data practices. The latest version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the official NOVA contact method provided with the current product or website.
