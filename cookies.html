1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies can include local storage and session storage.

2. Essential storage

The current website may use local browser storage to remember that you dismissed the cookie notice. This is a functional preference, not an advertising profile.

3. Optional analytics

If NOVA adds analytics, measurement tools should be identified here, including the provider, purpose, categories of information, retention and available controls.

4. Advertising

No advertising cookies should be represented as enabled unless advertising technology is actually deployed and the appropriate notice and consent mechanism is in place.

5. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some storage can affect website functionality.
