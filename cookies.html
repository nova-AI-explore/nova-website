Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of information stored by a browser. Similar technologies may also be used for preferences, security or measurement.

2. Essential technologies

Some storage may be necessary for the website to function, remember a preference, protect forms or maintain basic security. These technologies are not used for optional advertising simply because they are present.

3. Analytics

If NOVA adds analytics or other optional measurement tools, the website will identify the relevant categories and provide controls where required by applicable law.

4. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Where the site uses optional cookies that require consent, the NOVA cookie controls can be used to change your choices.

5. Updates

This policy may change when website technologies change.
